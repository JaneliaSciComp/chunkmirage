Register in the browser

Aligns a moving image onto a fixed one in two steps: an affine places it roughly (scale, rotation, shift), given or found here, then this computer's GPU fits a smooth field of local shifts on top. The images are read straight from their URLs and nothing is uploaded; the registered volume is computed here, chunk by chunk, as the viewer asks.
